## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an avant-garde cinema intelligence hub, merging high-theatrical widescreen drama with technical, developer-grade precision. The emotional tone balances the atmosphere of a soundstage projection room with the cold, lucid certainty of a deep-learning analysis engine. 

The aesthetic is constructed on an obsidian bedrock, punctuated by electric cyan luminescences and hyper-controlled structural lines. Key tenets:
- **Atmospheric Obsidian:** Deep, calibrated negative space that mimics high-contrast projection screen margins.
- **Spectral Intelligence:** Electric cyan, violet, and indigo light vectors denote synthetic machine cognition and predictive weighting.
- **Architectural Austerity:** Display type carries broad, dramatic letterforms countered by crystalline, monospaced-adjacent data hierarchies.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict darkness, preserving pure optical contrast and high black-point fidelity.

- **Primary (`#06B6D4` - Electric Cyan):** The primary cognitive beacon. Applied to active states, match indicators, high-confidence metadata vectors, and primary visual anchors.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6` - Deep Violet / Indigo):** The machine intuition channel. Applied to deep recommendation pathways, generative clusters, and subtle glow gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Projection Amber):** Expressly reserved for critical film evaluations, audience ratings, and historical awards marks.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#0B0D13` to `#121520`):** An obsidian scale calculated to maintain depth without pitch-black clipping. Cards sit on an elevated `#121520` base; canvas sits on `#0B0D13`. Borders leverage semi-translucent layers (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) to suggest laser-etched precision.

## Typography

The typography pits theatrical display typography against ultra-reduced, structural documentation type.

- **Headlines (`Syne`):** Used for heroic statements, movie titles, and thematic curations. Unconventional geometric curves project an avant-garde, festival-grade character.
- **Body & Data (`Geist`):** Delivers clean structural alignment, dense computational telemetry, runtimes, cast listings, and predictive algorithmic explanations.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered in uppercase `Geist` with wide letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to create technical telemetric stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an asymmetric, widescreen fluid grid modeled after 2.39:1 aspect ratio film framing.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on desktop (>1024px), 8-column layout on tablet (768px–1023px), and 4-column layout on mobile (<768px).
- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop sections breathe with deep margins (`3rem`) to frame visual artifacts, while mobile tightens down to `1.25rem` to maximize horizontal real estate for media carousels.
- **Film Strip Overflow:** Horizontal scrolling containers break gutters on mobile and tablet, bleeding seamlessly off-edge to indicate continuous lateral discovery.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial separation is achieved through transmissive glass and localized light conduits rather than drop shadows.

- **Obsidian Plinths:** Base surfaces use `#0B0D13`. Elevated cards and drawers occupy `#121520` at 70% opacity backed by a 24px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`).
- **Subtle Luminescent Borders:** Containers maintain thin, hairline boundaries (`1px`) styled with high-aperture gradients: top-left corner catches low-opacity cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)`), tapering to deep obsidian (`rgba(18, 21, 32, 0)`).
- **Photonic Glows:** Focused elements deploy an ambient outer plume: `box-shadow: 0 0 32px -8px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`. No heavy black drop shadows are permitted; all shadows mimic light dispersion on a dark floor.

## Shapes

The interface adopts a controlled, minimal radius model (`roundedness: 1`), keeping the visual hierarchy sharp and architectural.

- **Standard Elements (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to poster frames, metric tiles, interactive buttons, and input boundaries.
- **Card Containers (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to preview panels, flyouts, and modal windows.
- **Pill Exception:** Match badges and machine status tags are explicitly fully rounded (pill geometry) to distinguish mathematical signals from architectural content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`) background with `#0B0D13` heavy text. On hover, a soft `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)` perimeter bloom expands without scale distortion.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent obsidian base, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text in white. Focus transitions the border to solid `#06B6D4`.

### AI Match Badges
- Pill-shaped enclosure. Dark cyan-tinted glass background (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`) with a 1px contour of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`. Type is set in `label-xs` Geist, colored in `#06B6D4`, paired with a pulsing radial micro-indicator dot.

### Cinema Cards
- Frosted obsidian container (`#121520` at 80% opacity, 20px blur).
- Aspect ratio anchored to 16:9 for streaming feeds or 2:3 for theatrical keys.
- Subtle luminescent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). On hover, the border illuminates to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)` with an ambient violet-indigo backlight flare behind the poster art.

### Input Fields
- Deep charcoal fill (`#0E1118`), flush 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), crisp square-leaning corners (`0.25rem`).
- Active state draws a sharp Electric Cyan baseline underline and shifts the internal text color to high-contrast white.

### Ratings & Metadata Chips
- Displayed with tertiary Projection Amber (`#F59E0B`) text and matching low-opacity backgrounds (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`).
- Paired with strict monospaced runtime chips (e.g., `138 MIN`, `4K HDR`, `DOLBY ATMOS`) set in `label-xs` Geist at 50% opacity.

### Neural Cluster Inspector (Specialized Component)
- A bespoke AI exploration pane showing cinematic attribute vectors (e.g., "Atmospheric Dread: 94%", "Visual Symmetry: 88%"). Rendered as horizontal micro-gauges in high-luminosity Violet-to-Cyan gradients with numerical data right-aligned in `label-xs`.
